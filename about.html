What is FiberHance™?

FiberHance™ uses small molecules that penetrate deep into the hair cortex, the inner core where hair strength is decided. Ordinary products only coat the surface.

Heat, chemical treatment, stress and everyday wear weaken the keratin bonds that give hair its shape and elasticity. FiberHance™ helps reinforce and rebuild those bonds, so hair becomes stronger and more resilient over time.

"A new and unique bond-building technology that goes beyond the mechanism of regular hair care products, working by creating additional hydrogen and ionic bonds with the hair keratin structure." — Gold Innovation Award citation

Our Story

A Pharmacist Who Couldn't Solve Her Own Hair Loss

The Turning Point

4 years of postpartum hair loss, with no real answer

After childbirth, our founder lost hair for more than four years. Despite 11+ years in pharmacy, nothing worked: not pharmacy products, supplements, salon treatments or professional services. If even a pharmacist couldn't solve it, what about everyone else?

The Discovery

Going deeper than the surface

She studied Trichology through an international programme based in London and learned that most hair loss isn't only surface damage. It's structural breakdown inside the hair fibre. The cortex needed to be treated, not just the cuticle.

The Breakthrough

Bond-building technology that worked

She found FiberHance™, validated by Durham University and recognised with a Gold Innovation Award. After applying this approach herself, her four-year hair loss finally stabilised.

The Mission

Tested on 300 people before launch

She worked with a doctor and a cosmetic chemist to develop NISRAA through rigorous R&D. In a pilot study of 300 participants, 97% reported visible improvement. Only then was NISRAA launched.

For optimal results, use NISRAA as a complete treatment system:

🧴 Shampoo

Apply on wet hair and scalp. Leave for 2 minutes to allow the active technology to work. Rinse thoroughly. Use every other day.

🧴 Conditioner
